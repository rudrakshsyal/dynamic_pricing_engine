Model chosen for each sub-component and the rationale behind it
Component Model Key driver for this choice Outcome
AOG rate Seasonal mean Mechanical failures are random — no predictable trend or seasonality. Prophet found no signal and overfit noise. Simplest model wins on RMSE. Baseline wins
SMX rate Hybrid 2-week schedule visibility → use actual events within window; Prophet beyond. In-window actuals recalibrate the out-window forecast. +18.4% vs baseline
UMX rate Seasonal mean Unscheduled maintenance is noisy. Prophet overfit and was −13.3% vs baseline. Random signal — honest to use the simplest model. Baseline wins
CNA rate Constant 1.84% Crew unavailability showed no seasonality or trend in data. Constant rate is the most parsimonious model that fits the data-generating process. Constant wins
Program legs XGBoost Dominant signal is current booking count — encodes the booking curve directly. XGBoost with callout, DOW, peak flags, confirmed booking count beats pure time-series. +6.3% vs baseline
Non-prog legs Seasonal mean Low volume, weak signal. Prophet showed −0.8% vs baseline — essentially identical. Simpler model preferred at equal performance. Baseline wins
Design principle: Each sub-model was chosen by the simplest approach the data supported. Where complex models improved accuracy (SMX, Program legs), they were used. Where they didn't, the baseline was kept. This minimises overfitting risk across 6 independently composable and testable components.
