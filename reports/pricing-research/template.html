What stayed fixed in the GPT pipeline?

Model families, settings and selection rules. Six of seven daily components retained current recipes. Scheduled maintenance used ridge with a 1,095-day lookback; conversion used isotonic regression.

Models refit before each test using available past data. Refitting parameters is not model reselection.

Why quarterly windows?

Twelve origins from December 31, 2032 to September 30, 2035 cover seasons and summer maintenance shocks. Each predicts 90 days: 1,080 test dates per path.

Three seed42 January windows were previously examined. This is expanded robustness testing, not an entirely untouched holdout.
06 / Interpretation

What we can—and cannot—conclude.

How synthetic data were created

Weekday and annual patterns were fitted to the two real years. Joint 28-day residual blocks and small perturbations generated new daily series. Historical monthly distributions generated enquiry yields, booking outcomes, and lead times. Three seeds vary the draws; paired stress paths add 25% enquiry growth or summer maintenance increases (+8 points scheduled, +4 points unscheduled).

What the agent optimized

Component forecast RMSE, quote-multiplier bin error, and conversion log loss, with discretion for stability and simplicity. It did not jointly optimize remaining supply, pricing decisions, or revenue.

Why improvement is not consistent

Validation gains did not reliably transfer. Components interact through clipped remaining supply and the nonlinear pricing solver. Better component scores need not improve prices, and aggregate error can improve even when a component worsens.

Limits and next decision

Because the simulator preserves historical statistical patterns and the pricing reference uses current curves, the evaluation may favor aspects of the existing pipeline. The size of that effect has not been established. Synthetic histories expand scenario coverage, not independent real evidence. No fresh operational data are available. No revenue uplift or statistical significance has been established. Keep current ML as the default. A future Gemini comparison should use matched tools, budgets and test conditions; Gemini has not been tested here.

04 / Reliability

Checks before confidence.

Implemented safeguards

Failed evidence checks block AI use. The explanation validator requires all three tools, recognized citations, three short findings, and numerically grounded claims. Invalid generated output can receive one correction attempt.

The original saved calculation remains available if the explanation fails.

What those checks do not prove

Citation and numeric checks are not a general guarantee of semantic correctness. Chat validates retrieval, references, length, and missing-data handling; it does not apply the full brief validator.

Human review remains necessary. This report does not claim measured explanation accuracy or production readiness.
Evidence is reconstructed from current saved files, not an original execution trace. File hashes identify the snapshot; they do not establish a historical engine run. Observational conversion and enquiry-date/departure-date alignment remain engine limitations.
